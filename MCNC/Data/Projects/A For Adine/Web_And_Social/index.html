Our Mission

Direct Feeding Programs

Every dollar goes straight to purchasing nutritious meals for children who would otherwise go hungry. We work with local suppliers to ensure fresh, culturally appropriate food reaches the kids who need it most.

School Fee Coverage

Education is the pathway out of poverty. We pay school fees directly to institutions, ensuring children can attend class without the burden of unpaid tuition hanging over their families.

Zero Infrastructure Waste

We do not build schools. We do not construct facilities. We do not fund administrative buildings. Every cent is allocated to the two things that matter: feeding children and keeping them in school.

Radical Transparency

Where Your Money Goes

The Chief Madothi Fund operates on a zero-overhead model. 100% of public donations go directly to children. Administrative costs are covered separately by private benefactors.

  • Food purchases: Direct payment to local suppliers
  • School fees: Direct payment to educational institutions
  • No salaries paid from donation funds
  • No building projects
  • No equipment purchases
  • No vehicle acquisitions

Verification Protocol

Every transaction is documented with receipts, photographs, and school enrollment confirmations. Quarterly reports are published with full financial breakdowns and impact metrics.
