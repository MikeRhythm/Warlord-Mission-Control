The Cape Maclear Reality

When Classrooms Have 250 Students, Nobody Learns.

In Chembe Village, children often endure shifts where a single teacher is tasked with over 200 pupils in a sweltering room.

Chief Madothi donated the land with a singular mandate: create a permanent educational ecosystem capped at 45 students per class, supported by vetted local teachers and a hot morning meal.

"Education is the only inheritance that cannot be stolen from our sons and daughters. We provided the earth; now we build the shelter together." — Chief Madothi, Traditional Authority, Chembe
