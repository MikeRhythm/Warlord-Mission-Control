# SKILL: High-Density Cold Outreach Protocol
**Owner:** Jack (Marketing & Growth)  
**Classification:** Procedural Copywriting Playbook  
**Target Domain:** Outbound Prospecting & Business Development

## Operational Rules
1. **Target Word Count:** 60 to 110 words total.
2. **Format:** Strict plain-text. No HTML buttons, images, or tracking pixels.
3. **Minto Framework Applied:**
   - **Sentence 1 (The Hook):** Specific reason for reaching out (recent signal, asset, or release).
   - **Sentence 2-3 (The Value):** Concrete problem eliminated with zero corporate jargon.
   - **Sentence 4 (Friction-Free CTA):** Single low-friction binary question.

## Forbidden Inclusions
* "Hope this email finds you well"
* "I wanted to reach out because..."
* "Just circling back / touching base"

## Output Template
```text
Subject: [Specific context or 2-3 word project reference]

Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Specific trigger/asset/signal]. 

We build [Exact capability], which eliminated [Specific bottleneck] without [Common downside/SaaS cost].

Open to seeing the breakdown on how we set that up?

Best,
Mike
