Introducing Chief Madothi’s Children’s School and Feeding Fund 

In Chembe Village on the shores of Cape Maclear (Monkey Bay, Mangochi), families work hard to give their children a brighter future. 

Together with Chief Madothi and community members Robert Matewere and William his nephew, we are launching the Chief Madothi’s Children’s School and Feeding Fund to meet two urgent needs: keeping children in school and making sure they don’t learn on an empty stomach. 

What your support does 

Covers children’s school fees so learners can enroll and stay in class—along with essentials like uniforms, exercise books, pens, and exam fees and extra computer classes. 
Run a simple, reliable feeding program that provides nutritious meals sourced from local markets, improving concentration, attendance, and health. 
 

Community-led, transparent, and accountable 

This initiative is locally guided by Mr Robert Matewere, and William his nephew, with community volunteers tracking beneficiaries, receipts, and meal schedules. We’ll share straightforward updates— how many learners are covered, how many meals served, and a plain-language summary of costs—so every donor can see their impact. 

 

How to help 

A small contribution goes a long way in Chembe Village. Monthly donations keep learners in school and meals on the table; once-off donations help us respond to urgent gaps. If you can’t donate then please share this appeal with someone who might or post it on your social media. 

Together, we can turn consistent support into consistent opportunity—one set of school fees and one meal at a time.
