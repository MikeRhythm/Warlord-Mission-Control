### 1. CORE NUGGET
Dollar For is a US-based non-profit organization that helps individuals reduce or completely forgive hospital medical bills by assisting them in applying for financial assistance programs offered by hospitals. The service is free, quick, and easy, and is 100% funded by generous donors.

### 2. CODE / IMPLEMENTATION
**Website:** `www.dollarfor.org`
**Eligibility Check Form Fields:**
- Hospital State (Select State)
- Original Bill Date (Select Bill Date)
- Total Owed to Hospital ($)
- Household Annual Income ($)
- Size of Household (1, 2, 3+ People)
- Insurance Status (Insured, Uninsured)
**Process Steps:**
1.  See if you qualify, then fill out our patient form.
2.  We'll prepare your application for you and send it to the hospital.
3.  We verify with you and the hospital to get you medical debt relief.

### 1. CORE NUGGET
GoFundMe is a global crowdfunding platform that allows individuals to raise money for various personal causes, charities, and creative projects. It supports fundraising for unexpected medical emergencies, funeral costs, family circumstances, community causes, and more. The platform is free to use, and funds are disbursed even if the full goal is not met (unlike all-or-nothing platforms).

### 2. CODE / IMPLEMENTATION
**Website:** `www.gofundme.com`
**Fundraising Setup Steps:**
1.  Choose your country (e.g., United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Luxembourg, Mexico, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Belgium, Denmark, Austria).
2.  Enter your postcode/zip code.
3.  Select the cause area (e.g., Animals, Business, Community, Competitions, Creative, Education, Emergencies, Environment, Events, Faith, Family, Funerals & Memorials, Medical, Monthly Bills, Newlyweds, Other, Sports, Travel, Ukraine Relief, Volunteers, Wishes).
4.  Select who you are fundraising for (Yourself, Someone else, Charity).
5.  Enter your starting goal amount (e.g., £5,000).
6.  Automated goal setting (optional, gradually adjusts goal to build momentum).
7.  Sign in/Sign up to create an account.
**Key Considerations for Fundraisers:**
- Be clear about: what happened, what you need, how much you need, and what the money will be used for.

### 1. CORE NUGGET
Turn2Us is a UK-based charity that helps vulnerable people access financial support through grants and benefits. It is not a crowdfunding platform but rather a search tool that matches individuals with existing grants they may be eligible for. Eligibility is based on factors like income, household composition, and specific needs, not necessarily requiring benefit receipt.

### 2. CODE / IMPLEMENTATION
**Website:** `www.turn2us.org.uk`
**Grants Search Process:**
1.  Take a few minutes to answer some questions (information is anonymous and not shared).
2.  We'll match your information to grants that you may be able to apply for.
3.  You can then choose which grants you want to apply to.
**Application Process:**
1.  Click "Get Started".
2.  Answer questions about yourself (age, gender, etc.).
3.  Provide consent for data usage.
4.  Answer questions about your household income, child's disability benefits, support learning plan, professional reports, etc.
**Support Areas:** Heating oil, crisis payment, financial resilience, housing payment, community coordination, holiday activities and food programs, district council crisis and resilience fund support.

5.  Apply directly or via third-party links.
**Support Areas:** Increasing bills and cost of living, loss of work/income/other financial shock, universal credit cuts, essential household items, medical equipment, specialist equipment, technology, family breaks, play/sensory equipment, practical support.

### 1. CORE NUGGET
Family Fund is a UK charity that provides grants to families raising disabled or seriously ill children and young people (aged 0-17). Grants cover a range of needs including medical equipment, essential household items, technology, family breaks, and play/sensory equipment. Eligibility criteria include income thresholds and the child's support needs.

### 2. CODE / IMPLEMENTATION
**Website:** `www.familyfund.org.uk`
**Application Process:**
1.  Click "Can I apply?".
2.  Review eligibility criteria (child aged 0-17, parent/carer, right to work in UK, lived in UK for 6 months, low income/receiving benefits, child/young person needs high levels of support in 3+ areas of criteria).
3.  Click "Get started".
4.  Select country of residence (England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales).
5.  Select age group of disabled child (Under 8, 8-12, 13-17, None of these describe who I'd like to apply for).
6.  Provide consent for data usage.
7.  Start a new application (requires income and child details, supporting documents).
**Grant Examples:** Medical equipment, essential household items, specialist equipment, technology, family breaks, play/sensory equipment, practical support.
