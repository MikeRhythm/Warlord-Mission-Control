## 3. CORE PROGRAMS & OPERATIONAL TIMING

### Program A: Scholastic Retention (Admin: Robert Matewere)
* Term tuition and exam fee coverage paid directly to educational institutions.
* Provision of locally tailored uniforms, exercise books, pens, and mathematical kits.
* Supplementary computer literacy classes.

### Program B: Daily Nutrition Engine (Admin: William)
* **Execution Schedule:** Hot, nutritious meals served **at the end of the school day**.
* **Supply Chain:** 100% of grain, vegetables, and proteins sourced directly from local Chembe Village markets.
* **Impact Target:** Replenishing energy reserves after classes, incentivizing full-day classroom attendance, and eliminating afternoon domestic food stress.
