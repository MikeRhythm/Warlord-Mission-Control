Introducing Chief Madothi’s Children’s School and Feeding Fund 

In Chembe Village on the shores of Cape Maclear (Monkey Bay, Mangochi), families work hard to give their children a brighter future. 

Together with Chief Madothi and community members Robert Matewere and William his nephew, we are launching the Chief Madothi’s Children’s School and Feeding Fund to meet two urgent needs: keeping children in school and making sure they don’t learn on an empty stomach. 

What your support does 

Covers children’s school fees so learners can enroll and stay in class—along with essentials like uniforms, exercise books, pens, and exam fees and extra computer classes. 
Run a simple, reliable feeding program that provides nutritious meals sourced from local markets, improving concentration, attendance, and health. 
 

Community-led, transparent, and accountable 

This initiative is locally guided by Mr Robert Matewere, and William his nephew, with community volunteers tracking beneficiaries, receipts, and meal schedules. We’ll share straightforward updates— how many learners are covered, how many meals served, and a plain-language summary of costs—so every donor can see their impact. 

 

How to help 

A small contribution goes a long way in Chembe Village. Monthly donations keep learners in school and meals on the table; once-off donations help us respond to urgent gaps. If you can’t donate then please share this appeal with someone who might or post it on your social media. 

Together, we can turn consistent support into consistent opportunity—one set of school fees and one meal at a time. 

 

Thank you so much for your support…… 

 

Chief Madothi, Mr. Robert R. Matewere, William, Mike and Adine

Email:  chiefmadothifund@gmail.com

Chief Mado t hi's Children's School and Feeding Fund A Fundraising Initiative by Chief Madothi , Robert Matewere &amp; William With the fundraising support of Mike &amp; Adine who would prefer to be anonymous and would prefer to draw your attention to the real , incredibly worthwhile efforts of Mr. Robert Matewere , his nephew William and other community members. Chembe Village—nestled at Cape Maclear on the southern shores of Lake Malawi in the Mangochi District of Malawi —is a place where the lake sets the rhythm of daily life. Fishermen push out their wooden boats at first light, children walk to school along sandy paths, and evenings end with some of Africa’s most unforgettable sunsets over clear, island-dotted water. Cape Maclear was named in the 19th century (after astronomer Sir Thomas Maclear), and today Chembe remains a close-knit community whose livelihood depends on fishing, small-scale farming, and the growing trickle of eco-tourism. This campaign supports local efforts to keep children in school and ensure they are well-nourished—simple interventions that make a lasting difference. Your contribution helps cover school fees and supplies, strengthens a community-run feeding program, and backs the people who quietly keep it all moving. Please note : That this Fund is currently not a registered NPO with the Malawi Authorities , however we will be register ed when funds are available to pay the MK 253,500 registration fee . About the Fund and Its Administrators While living in Cape Maclear, Mike and Adine were fortunate to meet Mr. Robert R Matewere, who is dedicated to ensuring local children receive an education , protection and support . Robert raises funds to cover school fees, uniforms, books, pens, pencils, and exam fees. He also ensures that the children attend school and that all funds are used appropriately. Robert serves as the official administrator of the fund, and his Malawi Identity Card is attached for verification. Working alongside him is his nephew, William, who has taken the initiative to run a children's feeding program. William, with the support of other community members, ensures that the funds raised go directly to feeding the children. His Malawi Identity Card is also attached for verification. Mike and Adine were so impressed by Robert and William's dedication that they decided to help them raise funds to support their incredibly worthwhile efforts . In essence, these are the costs involved in educating and fee ding a child in the village. School Term Schedule The school term schedule helps to ensure transparency and allows donors to see when fees and meals are required most. School Fees Support Education is the key to opportunity. Funds raised are used to cover: - School fees - Uniforms - Exercise books, pens, pencils - Exam fees Mr. Robert Matewere oversees the proper use of all funds. Feeding Program Initiative No child should go hungry at school. William, Robert's nephew, together with the support of other community members, ensures that children are provided with nutritious meals during the school day , locally sourced from the village market . This program helps children concentrate better in class and supports their health and wellbeing. Additional Photos Thank You We are deeply grateful to all supporters and donors who make this program possible. Together, we can turn consistent support into consistent opportunity and ensure that the children in Cape Maclear have access to both education and nutrition —one set of school fees and one meal at a time. Thank you so much for your support…… __________________________________ Mr. Robert R. Matewere, Chief Madothi , William, Mike and Adine
