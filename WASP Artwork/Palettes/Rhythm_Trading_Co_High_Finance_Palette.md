**Theme:** High Finance // Wall Street Luxury

---

### Roxy's Brand Identity Sitrep
Visual architecture designed for the Command Center and Client Portal:

* **The "Foundation" (Black / Dark Charcoal):**
  * Hex: #121212 (Midnight Charcoal with subtle Obsidian matte finish).
  * Purpose: Deep ground base providing contrast without the flat, cheap look of standard tech black.

* **The "Luxury Accents" (Dark Gold):**
  * Hex: #C5A059 (Brushed Antique Gold / Vintage Patek Philippe tone).
  * Purpose: Reserved for high-value metrics (Total Equity, Premium Status) and primary CTA borders.

* **The "Reflective Layer" (Perspex & Silver):**
  * Material: Glassmorphism -- semi-transparent frosted glass widget cards.
  * Border: 0.5px Silver Beveled edge with dynamic specular scroll highlights.

* **The "Atmosphere" (Good Lighting):**
  * Lighting: Faint, warm radial glow gradients (Goldenrod halo) behind critical metrics.
